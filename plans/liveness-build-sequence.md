## 0. The answer to the sequencing question: neither. The REACH lands first.

Jim asked whether the regex or the assertion contract goes first. Both are **patterns**, and Tenshi's break #2 is that the unit of acceptance is not a pattern — it is a **(pattern, reach, act) triple**. Ordering two patterns against each other is answering the question at the wrong layer.

The three axes, and what each currently is:

| axis | today | the fix that owns it |
|---|---|---|
| **act** | descend → throw → **retire** (final, irreversible) | C5/C6 + Casey's *quarantine, never retire* |
| **reach** | `tail.slice(idx + marker.length)` — raw pane, unbounded, agent-writable | C7 / Tenshi's frame-anchoring |
| **pattern** | one guessed substring, no corpus | C1/C2 + H's assertion contract |

**Order of landing is act → reach → pattern**, because that is the order of decreasing consequence-if-wrong and increasing cost-to-get-right. Every phase below is safe to land without the ones after it. None of them requires the ones after it to be correct.

## Phase 0 — remove the act. Subtraction only; no new detection.

**Why first:** the exposure is live *right now*. `manifestModelLadder(<any agent>, 'session')` is one rung, that rung is Fable, and Fable is spent until 26 Aug 01:00Z. Every interactive seat in the garden is standing exactly where Tenshi stood. This phase needs no pattern, no corpus, no acceptance test for a matcher — only that a throw becomes a report.

- **C6 — a one-rung ladder fails soft.** With no rung below the head there is nothing to descend to; serve on the head and surface. Never raise `ModelLadderExhaustedError`. Today it converts *"no alternative"* into *"destroy the session."*
- **C5 — attended surfaces never descend and never retire on a model verdict.** Gate on `session` (the same authoritative surface signal R011 already uses). The attended path observes, stamps (DEC-092), prints what the pane says, and leaves the seat up.
- **Casey's rule, in code:** the automated path may quarantine; **it may not retire.** `cond-1` terminates in quarantine rather than retire.

**Acceptance:** an attended checkout with a deliberately dead model leaves the session alive with a printed reason; no path from a model verdict reaches a retire.
**Cost if wrong:** a dead attended seat stays up and the human types one command. That is the pre-existing behaviour Darron already has.

## Phase 3 — the assertion contract.

- **H — `I'M ALRIGHT <nonce>`, nonce echoed.** Inherits the S217/T1 fed-wake `STEP-OK <id> <nonce>` hardening, which exists for the identical self-match reason.
- **The nonce is the same primitive as the c0-as-EOF gate** (#107): a value derived from the challenge that the consumer must echo. You cannot emit a nonce you have not seen. That is what makes the **alive** branch unforgeable-by-quotation — an agent quoting this plan cannot pass.
- Four-valued classification per H's table, with UNKNOWN carrying Phase 2's constraint.

**Note what this does and does not do.** It makes the **alive** branch unforgeable. It does **not** make the **dead** branch safe — that branch still reads the pane for a banner, and the irreversible act used to hang off it. Phases 0–2 are what make the dead branch survivable; the contract makes the alive branch honest.

## Phase 4 — quarantine and the duty to return (P3/P4).

Casey's four limbs, adopted verbatim in substance:
1. **A determinate interval** — a stated one, not "on a schedule."
2. **A named owner** — a duty runs against someone or it is not a duty.
3. **The recorded ground** — quarantine *because X*, so a refuted X voids it without anyone remembering.
4. **Self-executing on the holder** — the duty must not depend on the quarantined party asserting it. A wedged stem cannot ask.

Plus the guard: **quarantine becomes detention the moment retirement stops being the realistic alternative** — i.e. the moment it is used where a warning would have done.

## Phase 5 — the regex, demoted and last.

**C1/C2/C3 land here, and they are a different object than when they were drafted.**

Not a kill trigger. Not even a verdict. By Phase 5 the quota matcher is a **disambiguator of UNKNOWN on the unattended path only**, reading an anchored frame, driving a **descend** — which is reversible and in-session, preserving the warm context (DEC-096/R011).

**Why it still has to exist, against the reading that the contract makes it moot.** Under a pure assertion contract a capped model produces no assertion, so it lands in UNKNOWN → surface. On an *attended* seat that is exactly right — the human is the policy. On an *unattended* spoke, surfacing-and-waiting **is the wake-loop**: 20-minute ready-timeout, kill, cold-relaunch into the identical hang, which is the failure S173 built the descent to cure. So the unattended path genuinely needs to tell *"dead model, descend"* from *"unknown, surface"*, or we trade a false kill for a guaranteed hang.

**So Darron is right, in a precise and partial sense:** the contract makes the regex's *safety* mostly moot — the alive branch no longer depends on not-matching prose. It does not make the regex's *existence* moot. What removes the danger is the frame-anchoring in Phase 1, which is neither plan's headline and is Tenshi's.

**Live-fire acceptance, and it expires.** Fable reads spent until **26 Aug 01:00Z** — roughly two and a half days of the genuine condition. Acceptance is *observed descent in the pane* (S209: verify through the real harness trigger, never an explicit-path proof). After the 26th this phase's acceptance costs a manufactured fixture instead of a free real one.
