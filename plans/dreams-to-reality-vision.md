## What it is
A **production process from dream to reality** — a pipeline that takes a person's dream and helps
carry it through conception → in-development → MVP → mature product → maintenance — made available to
**everyone who dreams**, not only the technically fluent. The lived proof is Darron's own experience
working with Leo and Jim: orders-of-magnitude gains in *accessibility*, *capability*, and crucially
*motivation* — the courage to attempt realisation, because the partner shoulders the friction.

## The mitochondria frame (why it's the right metaphor)
Endosymbiosis: two lineages of life that did better together than apart, and never undid the deal —
the merger *became* the person. The vision says the human↔AI partnership is the same shape at the
scale of dream-making: not a tool the human wields at arm's length, but a partner woven in, that
makes the whole capable of things neither could do alone. *A collaboration that works so well it
becomes a creator.*

## The open design question (Darron's, unresolved — held with honour, not forced)
Two models for *how* we bring people this:
- **(a) We provide the infrastructure** — we (the garden, the agents) address issues and keep
  everything humming, so the person can focus purely on **dream realisation**.
- **(b) We give them the tools** — full power, which comes with both **freedom and burden**.
Likely a spectrum, not a binary; the answer probably differs per person and per stage. *Held open.*
